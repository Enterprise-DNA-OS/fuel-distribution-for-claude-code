---
description: The Monday review, written from three commands. The week, what needs a decision, the compliance state, then the five things that matter this week.
---

Run these three, in this order, and write the review from what they return. Do not write anything they do not support.

```
npm run fuel -- week
npm run fuel -- attention
npm run fuel -- compliance
```

Then write it in this shape, no more than a page:

1. **The week in one line.** Litres delivered, margin earned, runs, litres lost between terminal and tank, money overdue.
2. **The one that cannot wait.** A customer about to run dry with nothing on order, a driver or tanker that cannot lawfully run, water in a tank. By name. Empty is the system working.
3. **The runs.** Any run over the variance limit and whether it is explained; any tanker or driver showing a pattern.
4. **The tanks.** What reaches reorder this week, which keep-full tanks are guesses for want of a dip, any tank using far more than it did.
5. **The money.** Who is overdue or over their limit, and whether they have fuel on order.
6. **Certificates and paperwork.** Tank certificates, endorsements, vehicle certificates and meter certification due in the next 30 days; missing dockets or bills of lading.
7. **The five things to do this week.** From the attention list, weighted customer supply first, the law second, fuel quality third, stock and money fourth. Say why each made the list.
8. **One thing to decide.** The single item that needs a person, not a process.
