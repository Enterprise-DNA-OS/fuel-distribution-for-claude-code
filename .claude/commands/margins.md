---
description: Where the money is made and lost. Margin over the terminal gate price by customer, drops, average drop and margin per drop, smallest first. Plus the tanks using more than they did.
---

1. Run `npm run fuel -- margins [--days=30]`.
2. Lead with the bottom of the margin-per-drop list: the customers whose small drops cost a truck, a driver and an hour for little margin. Say what would change it (a bigger tank, a keep-full arrangement so drops are larger, a minimum drop, a delivery fee) without recommending a price.
3. Then run `npm run fuel -- usage`. Tanks using a quarter more than before are a leak, theft or a new machine: suggest a call to the customer. Tanks filled three or more times in four weeks want a bigger tank or a second one.
4. Numbers in tables, one line of reading per finding.
